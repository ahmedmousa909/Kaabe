Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Development-stage notice: KAABE is an independent AI automation project currently being developed and tested. Features and data practices may change before public launch. This document must be reviewed and completed with the operator's legally required identity and contact details before production use.

1. Who operates KAABE

KAABE is currently operated as a development-stage project by an individual developer based in Germany, with a planned focus on serving businesses in African markets. Contact: ahmedmouse909@gmail.com. The operator's full legally required identification and postal contact information must be added before a public production launch.

2. Information processed

Depending on which features are used during testing, KAABE may process account email addresses, authentication identifiers, business and assistant settings, workflow instructions, knowledge-base content, chatbot prompts and replies, automation execution records, and technical diagnostics. For WhatsApp integration testing, inbound webhook events may include sender identifiers or phone numbers, WhatsApp message identifiers, message text, timestamps and delivery or processing status.

3. Why information is used

We process information to authenticate users, provide and test AI assistants and automations, receive authorized customer messages, troubleshoot failures, protect the service, and respond to support or privacy requests. AI-generated responses can be inaccurate and should be reviewed before consequential use.

4. Service providers and international transfers

KAABE currently uses Supabase for backend and database services, GitHub Pages for website hosting, and OpenAI-powered functionality for certain AI features. Meta's WhatsApp Cloud API is used for WhatsApp integration testing. Information submitted to these features may be processed by the relevant providers under their applicable terms and settings. Processing locations and cross-border transfer safeguards must be verified and documented before wider deployment, especially when serving users in the EEA and Africa.

5. Legal basis and retention

Where the GDPR applies, the applicable legal basis depends on the processing activity, such as providing a requested service, legitimate interests in service security, or consent where required. Retention periods are still being defined for the production service; development records are retained only as needed for testing, troubleshooting and applicable obligations, subject to deletion requests and technical limitations. Specific retention schedules and legal bases must be confirmed before public launch.

6. Sharing and security

Information is shared with service providers as needed to run the requested features and may be disclosed when legally required. We use access controls and server-side handling of sensitive integration credentials, but no online system is completely secure. Do not submit sensitive or confidential personal information into development tests.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection to certain processing, and withdraw consent where relevant. To submit a request, see Data Deletion or email ahmedmouse909@gmail.com. You may also have the right to complain to a competent data protection authority.

8. Changes

We will update this policy when KAABE's functionality, vendors, data practices or legal details change. Material changes should be communicated appropriately before they take effect.
