Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Development-stage notice: KAABE is an independent AI automation project currently being developed and tested. Features and data practices may change before public launch. This document must be reviewed and completed with the operator's legally required identity and contact details before production use.

How to request deletion

You can request deletion of personal data associated with your KAABE testing account, messages or connected integrations by emailing ahmedmouse909@gmail.com with the subject KAABE Data Deletion Request.

  1. State which KAABE account, email address or WhatsApp number the request relates to.
  2. Describe whether you want an account, a particular message, or all associated personal data deleted.
  3. We may ask for limited additional information to verify that you are authorized to make the request. Never email passwords, access tokens or identity documents unless a secure method is specifically arranged.
  4. We will review the request and respond within the period required by applicable law. If some information must be retained for a legal obligation, we will explain that where required.

What may be deleted

Depending on the records associated with you, deletion may cover account details, business configuration, AI conversations, workflow records and stored WhatsApp inbound test events. Data already held independently by Meta, OpenAI or other third-party services may require separate requests to those providers.

Important limitation

This page describes an email-based request process. KAABE does not currently claim to provide an automated self-service deletion button. Disconnecting WhatsApp from KAABE does not itself guarantee deletion of previously stored messages.

Other privacy requests

You may use the same contact email for access, correction and other applicable privacy requests. See the Privacy Policy.
